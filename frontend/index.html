Core Research Objectives (RO1 - RO4)

RO1: Quantum Recognition

Recognize and classify complex neurological disorders using Quantum Machine Learning (QML) algorithms on High-Performance Computing (HPC) networks.

RO2: Multi-Modal Biomarker Analysis

Evaluate MRI images alongside clinical risk metrics: age, hypertension, glucose levels, BMI, smoking habits, and alcohol intake.

RO3: Classical vs. QML Benchmarking

Benchmark classical ML (CNN, Random Forest, Decision Tree, LR, SVM, KNN, GNB) against QML circuits to demonstrate quantum precision and efficacy.

RO4: User-Friendly Diagnostic Interface

Deploy an interactive healthcare dashboard for early disease detection, severity rating, and personalized preventive care recommendations.
